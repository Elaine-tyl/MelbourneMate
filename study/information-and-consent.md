# Participant information and consent

## About the study

We are two students building MelbourneMate, a prototype that answers questions
about settling in Melbourne using a small set of official sources. We want to
see how it compares with searching official websites.

## What you will do

You will try four short everyday tasks, such as finding out how many hours you
may work on a student visa. You will use MelbourneMate for two tasks and your
usual web search for the other two. Each task takes up to 10 minutes, so the
whole session takes about 45 minutes.

After each task we will ask how confident you are in your answer and how much
you trust the information, on a scale from 1 to 5.

## What we record

We record whether each task was completed, how long it took and your two
ratings. We give you a code such as P03 instead of using your name. We do not
record your name, visa details, contact details or screen.

The results appear only as group totals in our course report and
presentation.

## Your choice

Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any task or stop at any time without
giving a reason. MelbourneMate is a student prototype, so please do not rely on
its answers for real decisions. Check the official source instead.
